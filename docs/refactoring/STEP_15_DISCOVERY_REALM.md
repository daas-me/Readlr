# Step 15: Optional Discovery Realm

Completed 2026-10-13 as a development-only prototype.

## Delivered

- Storybook Library with a three-page, two-sided book presentation.
- Puzzle Workshop with a constrained `at` to `mat` word change.
- Reading Garden with voluntary, persistent-world-style decoration interaction.
- Adventure Trails with a short word-clue choice.
- Review Quest destination that waits for an approved later-review pack.
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts in the Learning Camp preview.

## Responsible engagement

The prototype has no daily streak, missed-day penalty, expiring reward, forced return, or screen-time target. Discovery activities do not change mastery, curriculum evidence, legacy stage completion, or earned rewards.

## Content boundary

The current text uses only the provisional `m-a-t` content set and is labeled for review. It is not educator-approved story content and is not available in ordinary learner accounts. Production release requires:

1. approved graduate eligibility based on valid curriculum evidence;
2. teacher-reviewed stories, questions, pictures, and decodability;
3. approved additional word groups;
4. a separate personal-world persistence contract;
5. accessibility and target-age usability review.

## Preview

Open `learning-camp-preview.html` and choose `Discovery Realm`. The preview reads no account data and writes no progress or evidence.
