Problems  or  Issues  Encountered  by  Users
 
●  Stage  1  challenges  felt  repetitive  over  extended  use,  reducing  engagement  the  longer  learners  stayed  
in
 
the
 
training

 ●  The  fallback  text-to-speech  voice  used  when  Milo's  preferred  voice  was  unavailable  sounded  robotic

 ●  Internet  connectivity  was  unstable  during  the  session,  interrupting  the  testing  flow  at  points.   ●  A  small  number  of  learners  flagged  the  sound/pronunciation  activities  as  the  hardest  part  of  the  app.

User  Suggestions  and  Recommendations  
●  Let  learners  customize  Milo  with  preset  colors.   ●  Allow  learners  to  decorate  Milo  with  stickers  earned  as  rewards  for  completing  challenges.   ●  Give  Milo  a  friendlier,  more  complete,  less  abstract  character  design.   ●  Introduce  more  diverse,  less  repetitive  challenges,  especially  in  Stage  1
