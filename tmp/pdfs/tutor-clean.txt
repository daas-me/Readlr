Week 1: Sessions 1-5
SEL and Pre-Reading Activities
From Hello to Let’s Go!
This first week is all about connection before instruction.
Before we start reading remediation, we focus on building
trust and comfort so learners know they are in the care of
someone who sees them, believes in them, and will help them
grow.
Together with students, set clear rules and routines—they
create a safe, predictable space where everyone can learn
with confidence. Co-create a class agreement to keep things
fair and manageable, and involve learners in shaping their own
environment. Let them help arrange the space, display their
drawings and writings, and surround the room with print-rich,
easy-to-read visuals for natural, independent practice.
Use this time to also ease into learning with pre-reading
activities—reading orientation, active listening, and alphabet
introduction—to prepare students for the journey ahead. By
week’s end, you’ll have a class that feels connected, engaged,
and ready to learn.

Blindfold one learner or have them close their eyes. Ask a classmate to speak softly
into either the learner's left or right ear. The learner must guess who spoke and
identify whether the voice came from the left or the right ear. Assist learners who
need additional support and continue encouraging them to practice.
